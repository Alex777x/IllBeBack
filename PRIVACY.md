# Privacy Policy for I'll Be Back

**Effective Date:** September 30, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 30, 2026

**I'll Be Back** ("the Application") is a free, open-source macOS menu bar keep-awake utility. Your privacy is 100% protected.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection

**I'll Be Back** **does not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal data, usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry.

- **No Analytics or Tracking SDKs:** The Application contains zero third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.
- **No Network Requests:** All sleep-prevention (`IOKit` power assertions), battery monitoring, and countdown timer operations run 100% offline on your Mac.
- **Local Preferences Only:** Your settings (such as duration preset, global hotkey, and battery threshold) are stored exclusively in your Mac's local `UserDefaults`.

---

## 2. Open Source Transparency

The complete source code for **I'll Be Back** is publicly available for inspection under the MIT License at [https://github.com/Alex777x/IllBeBack](https://github.com/Alex777x/IllBeBack).
